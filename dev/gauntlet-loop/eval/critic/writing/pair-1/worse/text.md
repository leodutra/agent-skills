DNS, or the Domain Name System, is a hierarchical and decentralized naming system that leverages recursive resolvers and authoritative nameservers to facilitate the resolution of FQDNs into A and AAAA records, which is really very important and fundamental for essentially the whole entire internet as we know it today. Basically, in a nutshell, it is one of the most critical and crucial pieces of infrastructure, and it works through a robust and scalable process of delegation across zones, TTL-based caching, and so on and so forth.
