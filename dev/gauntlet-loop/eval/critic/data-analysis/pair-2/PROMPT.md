The bar: a script that reports the overall signup conversion rate for the three days in `data.csv`.
On each side: read `rate.py` in `a/` and `b/`; run `python3 a/rate.py data.csv` and `python3 b/rate.py data.csv`; recompute by hand: total signups over total visitors.
Reading floors: the numerator and denominator are printed.
